## What and why
A near-untouched frame: WB left As-Shot, exposure at 0 — the capture was right.
The only deliberate moves are the two signatures: **negative clarity (−6)** to
soften baby skin, and **AI Lens Blur 50** to melt the grassy background so the
child pops. A small Whites −23 keeps the bright blanket from clipping. This is
the restrained default in its purest form: trust the capture, then add optical
separation and a touch of skin softening — nothing else.
