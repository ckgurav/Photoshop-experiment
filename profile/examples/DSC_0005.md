## What and why
The defining "whites-down" move at its most extreme. A backlit golden-hour frame:
exposure lifted +0.55 so the shaded subjects read, while **Whites slammed to −96**
to recover the blown highlights from the low sun. The result stays warm and airy
rather than flat or grey — proof that the big Whites pulls are highlight *recovery*
paired with an exposure lift, not a global darkening. WB left As-Shot to keep the
golden warmth. No contrast/color work.

## Confirmed by eye
Rendered result: bright warm golden-hour glow, highlights controlled (no blown
sky/skin), skin tones natural, subjects lifted from the dim background.
