## What and why
The hardest kind of frame in the set — direct overhead sun — rescued with
aggressive tone recovery: contrast dropped hard (−45), highlights pulled (−71)
to bring back the sari and bright background, and shadows opened fully (+100) so
the face under the harsh light reads. Exposure eased down −0.70 overall. This is
the exception that proves the "restraint" rule: when the light is genuinely
difficult they will move many sliders a lot — but every move is *corrective*
(reduce contrast, recover tone), never stylistic. Lens Blur 50 then softens the
house to lift the subject off the background.
