## What and why
This exact recipe is applied **uniformly across the whole Half Sari shoot**
(DSC_0006–0016 all identical) — a batch "finished gallery" look rather than a
per-frame edit. Adobe Vivid + the mild contrast/vibrance push gives rich, warm,
slightly punchy colour that flatters gold brocade and festive decor. It is the
deliberate counterpart to the restrained Adobe-Color default: reached for when a
themed set wants saturated, consistent colour. Note it is still *gentle* — +18
vibrance and +18 contrast, not a crushed HDR look, and still no HSL/grading.

## Confirmed by eye
Rendered result: golds and magentas are vivid and clean, background softly
blurred, no haloing or over-saturation. Reads as a cohesive decorative frame.
